The Name "Panchi"

"泮池"之名

From Ancient Ritual to Modern Campus

从古代礼制到现代校园

Origin溯源

In ancient ritual, the emperor's school was Biyong (water on four sides); a vassal's school was Pangong (water on three sides, shaped like a half-disk). Later, Confucian temples and academies adopted semicircular pools—symbolizing boundless learning.

古代礼制中，天子之学称"辟雍"，四面环水；诸侯之学称"泮宫"，东西南三面环水，形如半璧。后来孔庙、官学前多设半圆形泮池，寓意学无止境。

Allusion典故

The Book of Songs says: "Joyful by Panchi's waters, I gather celery." Ancient scholars would pick celery from the pool upon enrollment—hence "gathering celery" or "entering Pan" meant starting school. Panchi thus became a symbol of academia.

《诗经》有云："思乐泮水，薄采其芹。"古时学宫有泮水，入学可采水中之芹为菜，故古人称入学为"采芹""入泮"，泮池也成了学府的象征。

Today今义

Today's Panchi at XTBC carries on that tradition of revering learning. It is a scholarly sanctuary on campus—and a gentle footnote on every student's journey.

今日的西工商泮池，延续着这份崇学尚文的传统。它是校园里的书香圣地，也是莘莘学子求学路上温柔的注脚。
